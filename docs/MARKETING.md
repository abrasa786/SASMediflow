# How to take Mediflow to market

This is a plan to test, not a forecast. Prices and numbers are starting hypotheses; change them after the first ten conversations.

## 1. Who first

Small and mid-size **dental and multi-specialty clinics in India with 1–10 staff** that use a paper register, Excel and WhatsApp today. They feel the pain (missed follow-ups, double bookings, lost dues, stock-outs), decide quickly (the owner is the buyer), and multi-sitting treatment tracking is a real edge for dentistry. Pick **one city and one specialty** first.

## 2. What we are selling

"Your whole clinic in one place, in your name": booking, treatments, billing, stock and staff, with an assistant that does the typing, branded with the clinic's own logo, colour and web address. Lead with what is true in the product today:

1. **Branded as the clinic**: own logo, colour, web address or domain, and a named AI assistant.
2. **Treatments by sitting**: plans, dues, stock use per visit.
3. **AI that stays in its lane and in your data**: answers only clinic questions, stores nothing outside your database, deletable on demand, external AI off by default.
4. **Build what you're missing**: the AI Builder adds apps and fields with approval and undo.
5. **An assistant that does the work**: calendar, records and dashboards by chat; admins add flows and fields by describing them.
6. **Your own address**: `www.yourclinic.com`, or `www.yourclinic.<ours>`.
7. **Per-clinic data isolation that is tested**, not just promised.

Don't claim HIPAA/ABDM/ISO certification, uptime guarantees, or integrations you haven't built.

## 3. Pricing hypothesis

Free 30-day trial, no card. Then per clinic per month: **Starter** (1–3 users), **Clinic** (up to 10 users, treatments, purchasing, AI Builder), **Group** (multiple branches, priority support). Test roughly ₹999 / ₹2,499 / custom with 10 real clinics, then adjust. Annual plan at two months free.

## 4. Channels, in order of expected payoff

1. **Founder-led demos**: visit 30 clinics in one area with a laptop and the 90-second video; ask for 15 minutes; offer to set the clinic up free during the trial.
2. **Dental and doctor associations and WhatsApp groups**: a short talk, "Stop losing follow-ups", with the demo.
3. **Referral**: one free month for each referred clinic that pays.
4. **Short video**: the 15 and 30-second cuts on Instagram, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp status.
5. **Local search**: a landing page per city and specialty, plus the public clinic pages (`/c/<slug>`) every customer gets.
6. **Dental depots and equipment reps**: they visit every clinic; offer a commission for introductions.

## 5. Funnel and what to measure

Demo booked → trial started → first appointment created → first invoice → active on day 7 → paid.
Weekly: demos, trials, activation (first booking within 3 days), 30-day retention, support tickets per clinic. Talk to every clinic that leaves.

## 6. First 90 days

| Weeks | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Staging walk-through of the video script; fix anything rough; record the video; 10 demos |
| 3–6 | 5 pilot clinics free for 60 days in exchange for feedback and a testimonial; weekly check-ins |
| 7–10 | Start charging pilots; add regional-language strings if asked for twice |
| 11–13 | Case study from the best pilot; referral offer; second area |

## 7. Trust as a selling point

Have these ready for every demo: `docs/SECURITY.md` on one page, a plain-language privacy note, the export and deletion promise, and Settings → AI & privacy on screen.
